An Ethereum block built inside an Intel TDX trust domain. Your browser checks the proof.

A block builder with no operating system under it, a unikernel written in Lean, was handed pending transactions and proofs of the state they touch. It chose which went in and in what order, ran them, and signed a bid for the block with a key that never leaves it. What it was asked and what it answered are below, with a quote signed by a key Intel certified. This page checks that the quote is for exactly those bytes, here in your browser.

    Measurements in the quote

    The processor measured the firmware as it loaded (MRTD) and the firmware measured what it then started (RTMRs). These are in the signed quote. What the operator says they belong to is below them, and is the operator's word.

    Read this before believing it

    What the checks say

    • A real Intel TDX trust domain, not in debug mode, with the measurements shown, gave this reply to this request.
    • It did so after the block's parent existed: the request was sent with the parent's hash as a challenge, and the quote covers it.
    • The reply names the block: its hash, what it pays the proposer, how many of the proposed transactions went in, and the builder's key and signature over the bid.

    What they do not say

    • Whether the block is on the chain. Look its hash up somewhere you trust; "landed" on this page is the operator's word.
    • Whether the builder's signature over the bid is good. This page does not check BLS signatures; bid-verify in the repository does.
    • That every transaction anyone knew of was offered. The host chooses what is proposed, and the transactions reach the guest in the clear: this builder keeps no order private.
    • That the measurements are the published source. For that, rebuild the image and boot it on a TDX host of your own. Nor does the browser ask Intel whether the processor's firmware is current or a certificate revoked.
    This page and the files it loads come from a server outside the trust domain, which is the untrusted host here. Only the quote and what it covers are evidence.
